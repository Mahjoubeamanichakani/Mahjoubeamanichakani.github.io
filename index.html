About Me

Mahjoube is a PhD candidate in Sociology at McGill University, specializing in Population Dynamics, with a strong interest in family trajectories and the transition to adulthood among immigrant populations. She holds a Master's degree in Demography and a Bachelor's degree in Social Planning, graduating among the top students in both programs. Her research examines how young immigrants navigate key life course transitions, including partnership formation, childbearing, and household formation, within a new cultural context. Her work seeks to demonstrate that living in a high-income country does not provide the same experience for immigrants as it does for the native-born, as immigrants often encounter unique social, economic, and institutional challenges during the settlement and integration process. With expertise in quantitative research methods, demographic analysis, and statistical modeling, she aims to advance the understanding of immigrant families and their life course trajectories. Her research is supported by the Fonds de recherche du Québec – Société et culture (FRQSC).
